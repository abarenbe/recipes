Restaurant Style Tofu Masala

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (454g) extra firm/firm tofu
  • 1 tsp chickpea flour (besan) for tofu rub
  • 1 tsp salt for tofu rub
  • 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika) for tofu rub
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder (haldi) for tofu rub
  • 3-4 tablespoons oil (e.g., vegetable, canola), divided
  • 1-2 tablespoons vegan butter (or regular butter/ghee), divided
  • 1-2 dried bay leaves
  • 1 small piece cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 green cardamoms
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika) for curry
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes (canned or fresh puree)
  • 1 tablespoon chickpea flour (besan) for thickening
  • 10 tablespoons coconut milk (or 2 heaped tbsp plain unflavored yogurt/cooking cream)
  • 2 whole green chilies
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Sugar, to taste
  • Water, as needed
  • Fresh coriander leaves, roughly chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Prepare Tofu: Remove tofu from package, discard water, and rinse under tap water. Wrap in paper towels, place on a plate, and press out excess moisture for about 30 minutes. Cut the pressed tofu into desired shapes (e.g., sticks, cubes), ensuring they are not too thin.
  2. Marinate Tofu: In a bowl, combine 1 tsp chickpea flour, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika), and 1/4 tsp turmeric powder. Mix well. Add the cut tofu to this spice rub and toss gently to coat all pieces. Set aside.
  3. Prepare Aromatics: Grate the ginger and garlic. Dice the large onion.
  4. Pan-Fry Tofu: Heat 2 tablespoons of oil and 1 tablespoon of vegan butter (or regular butter/ghee) in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated tofu pieces in a single layer, ensuring not to overcrowd the pan (fry in batches if necessary). Fry for about 2 minutes on each side until lightly golden and heated through, avoiding over-browning to prevent a rubbery texture. Remove the fried tofu to a bowl and set aside.
  5. Sauté Whole Spices & Onions: If needed, add 1-2 tablespoons of oil and 1 teaspoon of vegan butter (or regular butter/ghee) to the same pan. Add the whole spices: bay leaf, cinnamon stick, and green cardamoms. Stir for a few seconds to release their aroma. Add the diced onion and a pinch of salt. Sauté over medium heat for 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the onions are soft, shiny, and translucent (do not brown).
  6. Cook Ginger-Garlic & Dry Spices: Add the grated ginger and garlic to the pan. Stir for a few more minutes until the raw smell of ginger and garlic disappears. Reduce the heat to low. Add 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika) and stir for just a second to bring out its color. Add 2 tsp curry powder and stir for another second to release its flavor and aroma.
  7. Add Tomatoes & Thicken with Chickpea Flour: Pour in 1/2 cup of crushed tomatoes. Stir well. Rinse the tomato container with a little water (about 1/4 cup) and add to the pan. Stir for a few minutes. Cover the pan and cook on medium heat for 3-4 minutes. Uncover and stir. Sprinkle in 1 tablespoon of chickpea flour (besan). Stir on medium-low heat for a few minutes until the flour is cooked and releases a nutty flavor, thickening the gravy.
  8. Season & Add Liquids: Add salt and sugar to taste. Add about 1/4 cup of water, stir, then cover the pan and cook on medium-low heat for 5 minutes. Uncover and stir. Gradually add 10 tablespoons of coconut milk (or plain unflavored yogurt/cooking cream), stirring well between each addition until fully incorporated.
  9. Final Flavor Infusion: Add the 2 whole green chilies and 1/2 tsp garam masala powder. Stir. Add more water if a thinner gravy is desired, ensuring not to add too much to dilute the flavor. Stir well. Cover the pan and cook on medium-low heat for about 5 minutes.
  10. Combine Tofu & Finish: Uncover and stir. Taste the gravy and adjust seasonings if necessary. Add the pan-fried tofu pieces to the gravy. Gently stir to coat the tofu with the sauce. Cover the pan and cook for 3-4 minutes on low heat, allowing the tofu to absorb the rich flavors of the gravy.
  11. Serve: Discard the bay leaf before serving. Garnish with roughly chopped fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with Indian breads like roti or naan, pita bread, or rice.
