Cherry Yogurt Pie with Crumb Topping

Ingredients

  • 500 g (1.1 lbs) frozen cherries
  • 70 g (6 tbsp / 2.5 oz) cornstarch
  • 150 g (0.8 cups / 5 oz) sugar (for cherry filling)
  • 150 ml (0.7 cups / 5 oz) water
  • 3 egg yolks
  • A pinch of salt (for dough)
  • 80 g (6 tbsp / 2.8 oz) powdered sugar (for dough)
  • 80 g (1 stick) melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (for dough)
  • 200 g flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 500 g Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (for yogurt filling)
  • 50 g (4 tbsp / 1.7 oz) cornstarch (for yogurt filling)
  • 3 egg whites
  • A pinch of salt (for egg whites)
  • 130 g (0.7 cups / 4.5 oz) sugar (for egg whites)
  • Powdered sugar (for dusting)

Instructions

  1. Combine 500g frozen cherries, 70g cornstarch, 150g sugar, and 150ml water in a saucepan. Stir well to combine all ingredients.
  2. Cook the cherry mixture over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens. Set aside to cool.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together 3 egg yolks, a pinch of salt, and 80g powdered sugar until the mixture becomes light yellow and creamy.
  4. Add 80g melted butter and 1 tsp vanilla extract to the egg yolk mixture and whisk until thoroughly combined.
  5. Sift 200g flour and 1 tsp baking powder into the wet ingredients.
  6. Mix with a spatula until a cohesive dough forms.
  7. Separate approximately 1/4 of the dough, wrap it in plastic wrap, and place it in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  8. Line a 20 cm (8-inch) baking pan with parchment paper.
  9. Press the remaining larger portion of the dough evenly into the bottom and up the sides of the prepared baking pan, forming a crust.
  10. Pour the cooled cherry filling into the dough-lined pan and spread it evenly.
  11. In a clean bowl, whisk together 500g Greek yogurt, 1 tsp vanilla extract, and 50g cornstarch until smooth.
  12. In a separate clean bowl, beat 3 egg whites with a pinch of salt until foamy.
  13. Gradually add 130g sugar to the egg whites while continuing to beat until stiff peaks form.
  14. Gently fold the beaten egg whites into the Greek yogurt mixture until just combined, creating a fluffy yogurt filling.
  15. Pour the yogurt filling over the cherry layer in the baking pan and spread it evenly.
  16. Retrieve the small piece of dough from the freezer and grate it evenly over the top of the yogurt filling to create a crumb topping.
  17. Bake in a well-preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 40 minutes, or until the top of the cake rises evenly and turns golden brown.
  18. Once baked, remove the cake from the oven and let it cool completely before removing it from the pan.
  19. Dust generously with powdered sugar before serving.
